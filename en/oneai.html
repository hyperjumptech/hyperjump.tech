Why credits or stipends aren't enough

ChatGPT seats cost less for small teams. OneAI saves money once you need control, multiple models, and one budget for 20–40 people.

A fair objection: "We can give employees money, or buy credits ourselves." That makes sense for small teams with chat-only needs. As headcount grows, stipends often aren't used for AI, admin overhead increases, and features you need must be bought separately.

Not for you if

Fewer than 20 people with chat-only needs

For that team size, ChatGPT Plus or Business is usually enough and cheaper. OneAI isn't meant to replace personal subscriptions at that scale.

Good fit if

20–40 people who need one platform, not 20–40 subscriptions

One invoice of Rp12,400,000 for up to 40 users, including USD 300 credit. At this point, per-seat plus separate credits matches or exceeds OneAI — without ZDR, RAG, or model control.

Why OneAI

One platform beats three workarounds

Stipends, per-seat ChatGPT, and bolt-on tools each solve part of the problem. OneAI brings control, multi-model access, and enterprise features into a single budget — which is why it wins once your team outgrows ad-hoc AI.

Stipends often don't become AI spending

Money in employee accounts can be spent on anything. There is no model reporting, no audit trail, and no way to enforce company policy. You pay for capacity you cannot see.

Per-seat costs scale linearly

ChatGPT Business for 40 people ≈ Rp16 million, before extra credits. OneAI stays at Rp12.4 million for up to 40 users, with USD 300 credit included. The fuller the plan, the lower the cost per person.

Separate features cost more

RAG, office files, code execution, ZDR, and model control usually become separate projects or subscriptions. On OneAI they are one platform. That is why this plan can cost less even when the headline price looks higher than 10 ChatGPT seats.
